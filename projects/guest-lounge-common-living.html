Residential & Hospitality Lounge 2023 – 2024

Guest Lounge & Common Living

Hospitality-Infused Common Lounge & Family Living Pavilion

Delhi NCR, India
Design Vatika Architects
Architectural Narrative

Design Story & Execution

Serving as the social nucleus of a sprawling residence in Delhi NCR, this guest lounge redefines communal hospitality. Floor-to-ceiling timber louvers create visual separation between the foyer and main seating while maintaining uninterrupted line-of-sight and airflow.

The material palette is deeply rooted in earthy tones—warm terracotta, charcoal stone, brass trims, and saddle leather. A bespoke L-shaped sectional couch accommodates large gatherings, while satellite accent chairs create cozy reading clusters.

Bespoke artwork curated specifically for the project pays homage to Indian artisanal heritage, lit by museum-grade magnetic track spotlights.

Key Architectural Highlights

  • ✓Full-height rotating wooden louver spatial divider
  • ✓Museum-grade magnetic track lighting system for curated art
  • ✓Saddle-leather and performance-linen upholstery combination
  • ✓Integrated wet-bar and concealed beverage credenza
